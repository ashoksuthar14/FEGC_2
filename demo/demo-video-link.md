## Demo flow shown

1. **The lock screen.** A slip in play as an Android 16 Live Update: score,
   progress segments, the minute. The score is visible; the slip is not.
2. **The widget family.** Six home-screen widgets — Live slip, My club, While
   you were away, My season, My protection, and the fully club-themed card.
3. **The engine choosing silence.** "Why this?" renders the ledger: most events
   produce nothing, and every row says why.
4. **The narrator.** Same moment, four tones, two languages, written on-device.
5. **Missions.** "Set a deposit limit" — the mission no competitor ships.
6. **Rewards.** A redemption: terms, a code, no animation, no randomness.
7. **How it learns.** A notification swiped away, and the arm that sent it
   losing score for it.
8. **Casino.** The same engine producing a reality check.
